# Scenario set

## Contents
- Outcome range: best, base and worst
- Stress scenarios (pick at least six; all that apply)
- Format per scenario

## Outcome range

For each case give the numbers below, plus the one or two assumptions that make it that case
rather than another:
- customers or users at 6 and 12 months
- revenue
- cash burned
- months to the next gate

Anchor the base case to the reference-class base rates from preflight. The worst case is a real
failure path (no buyer, blocked by a regulation, runway out), not "slower growth".

## Stress scenarios

| # | Scenario | Prompts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Demand is weaker than believed** | Interviews were polite, not committed. What happens when the conversion rate is a fifth of the plan? |
| 2 | **Incumbent or platform response** | The biggest adjacent player ships this as a free feature. What is left? |
| 3 | **Key dependency lost or repriced** | An API, model provider, marketplace, data source or partner changes terms, price or access. |
| 4 | **Regulatory change** | A rule in the screen takes effect, tightens or is enforced, or a proposal becomes law. Use the dated items from `/legal-regulatory-compliance`. |
| 5 | **Funding drought** | No raise for 18 months. Is the project default-alive on its own revenue and runway? |
| 6 | **Cost shock** | Unit cost (inference, compute, acquisition, expert hours) doubles. Does the margin survive? |
| 7 | **Founder or key person unavailable** | Illness, job change, co-founder exit. What stops? |
| 8 | **Security or trust incident** | A breach, data leak, wrong output in production, or public failure. |
| 9 | **Success disaster** | Demand is ten times the plan. What breaks first: delivery, support, cash, quality? |
| 10 | **Timing slips** | The "why now" arrives two years late, or has already passed. |
| 11 | **Employer or IP challenge** | A current or former employer claims the work. |
| 12 | **Ethical or reputational backlash** | Who could be harmed, and how would that look in the press? |

## Format per scenario

`Trigger → what happens to the idea → early-warning signal (observable, with a date to check) →
pre-planned response → owner lens`. Mark likelihood (low/med/high) and impact (low/med/high).
Anything high on both becomes a kill criterion or a condition on the recommendation.
